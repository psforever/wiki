# Inventory Management

This mission will show you how to use lockers and set up inventory favorites.

**Where:** NC Sanctuary, TR Sanctuary, VS Sanctuary  
**Complete first:** [Weapon Handling](training_weapons01.md)  
**Unlocks:** [Vehicles](training_vehicles.md)  
**Reward:** 500 battle experience

## Walkthrough

### Step 1

> Learning how to manage your inventory can save you valuable time. In this mission, you will learn how to use lockers as well as saving and loading inventory Favorites.

### Step 2

> The lockers in Sanctuary are located in the HART building.
>
> Follow the waypoint to the HART building and go inside (or press <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Next**</span> if you are already inside one).

**Vanu Sovereignty**

> **Objective:** Follow the waypoints to the lockers

### Step 3

**Vanu Sovereignty**

> Head upstairs inside the HART building to the Observation Deck door.

### Step 4

**Vanu Sovereignty**

> Go through this door and down the hallway towards the next waypoint.

### Step 5

> Lockers can be used as additional storage space. Anything you store in a locker can be accessed in any other friendly locker. Outside of Sanctuary, lockers can be found in each base and tower, near the spawn rooms.
>
> Open one of the Lockers.

### Step 6

> Items can be moved to a locker by clicking and dragging an item in your inventory window to the locker window. You can also right-click an item and it will automatically get placed in the locker window. Try moving some items from your inventory to the locker.
>
> When you are finished, close the locker and open one of the nearby Equipment Terminals to learn about Favorites.

### Step 7: Save an inventory favorite

> Favorites can be used to quickly acquire specific loadouts. Configure your inventory space into something that you will be using often.
>
> When you are ready to make this into a Favorite, click on the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Favorites**</span> tab.

> **Objective:** Save an inventory favorite

### Step 8

> The <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Favorites**</span> tab is where you can select configurations to load and save.
>
> Select a favorite slot and click on the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Save**</span> button.

### Step 9: Load an inventory favorite

> Close the Equipment Terminal window.

> **Objective:** Load an inventory favorite

### Step 10

> Before you test the favorite you just saved, clear out your inventory.
>
> Open your inventory and press the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Clear**</span> button.

### Step 11

> Now that your inventory is cleared out, open an Equipment Terminal.

### Step 12

> Notice that when you have a Favorite saved, the Equipment Terminal automatically opens in the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Favorites**</span> tab.
>
> Select the Favorite you saved by pressing the corresponding number key on your keyboard.

### Step 13

> You should now have your favorite configuration loaded into your inventory.
>
> As you probably have learned, loading favorites can save you tons of time. You will also learn that favorites can be used to save vehicle weapon configurations as well.
>
> Press <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Next**</span> to complete this mission.

### Step 14

> Mission complete.
>
> Congratulations!  You have completed the Inventory Management training mission.
