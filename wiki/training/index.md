# Training Missions

The training missions built into the PlanetSide client, taken from the client files, in the order the client lists them. Some missions open only after you complete others.

- [Using the Map](training_map.md)
- [Basic Training](training_welcome.md)
- [Certifications](training_certifications.md) (requires [Weapon Handling](training_weapons01.md))
- [Healing and Repairing](training_healing.md) (requires [Weapon Handling](training_weapons01.md))
- [Implants](training_implants.md) (requires [Certifications](training_certifications.md) and battle rank 6)
- [Inventory Management](training_inventory.md) (requires [Weapon Handling](training_weapons01.md))
- [MAX and Infiltration armors](training_armors.md) (requires [Weapon Handling](training_weapons01.md))
- [Using Warpgates](training_warpgates.md) (requires [Using the Map](training_map.md))
- [Using the HART Shuttle](training_hart.md) (requires [Using the Map](training_map.md))
- [Vehicles](training_vehicles.md) (requires [Inventory Management](training_inventory.md) and [Weapon Handling](training_weapons01.md))
- [Weapon Handling](training_weapons01.md)
