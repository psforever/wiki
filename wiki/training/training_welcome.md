# Basic Training

This mission teaches how to equip yourself and get into battle.

**Where:** NC Sanctuary, TR Sanctuary, VS Sanctuary  
**Reward:** 500 battle experience

## Walkthrough

### Step 1: Use an equipment terminal

> This mission will show you how to equip yourself and get into combat.
>
> The first thing you need to do is get better equipment, because you won't last a minute with the gear you have right now.
>
> Find and use an <span style="color:#AACCFF">Equipment Terminal</span> now.
>
> Use your mouse to look around, and press <span style="color:#FFFFAA"><kbd>W</kbd> or <kbd>Numpad 8</kbd></span>, <span style="color:#FFFFAA"><kbd>A</kbd> or <kbd>Left Arrow</kbd></span>, <span style="color:#FFFFAA"><kbd>S</kbd> or <kbd>Numpad 2</kbd></span>, and <span style="color:#FFFFAA"><kbd>D</kbd> or <kbd>Right Arrow</kbd></span> to move.

> **Objective:** Use an equipment terminal

> **Objective:** Use an inventory favorite

### Step 2

> <span style="color:#AACCFF">Inventory Favorites</span> change your entire loadout instantly, and are the fastest way to get equipped.
>
> To continue, double-click one of the Favorites.

### Step 3: Switch to third person view

> **Objective:** Switch to third person view

### Step 4: Switch back to first person

> Press <span style="color:#FFFFAA"><kbd>T</kbd></span> to toggle between first person and third person views.
>
> Try switching to <span style="color:#AACCFF">third person</span> view now.

> **Objective:** Switch back to first person

### Step 5: Open your map

> Third person view can be especially useful when driving a vehicle.
>
> To continue training, press <span style="color:#FFFFAA"><kbd>T</kbd></span> again to return to <span style="color:#AACCFF">first person</span> view.

> **Objective:** Open your map

### Step 6

> Your map is vital to orienting yourself and staying aware of changes to the battlefield.  
>
> Press <span style="color:#FFFFAA"><kbd>M</kbd></span> to view the <span style="color:#AACCFF">map</span> now.

### Step 7: Toggle mouselook off

> The white arrow is your current location.  
>
> You can use the mousewheel to zoom in and out, and scroll by moving the mouse along the edge of the screen.
>
> To continue, press <kbd>Escape</kbd> to close the map.

> **Objective:** Toggle mouselook off

### Step 8: Toggle mouselook back on

> Occasionally you'll need to <span style="color:#AACCFF">toggle mouselook</span> off, so you can use the mouse cursor to interact with your HUD.
>
> Press <span style="color:#FFFFAA"><kbd>Tab</kbd></span> to toggle mouselook <span style="color:#AACCFF">off</span> now.

> **Objective:** Toggle mouselook back on

### Step 9: Open the Field Manual

> By turning mouselook off, you can use the cursor to move and resize many HUD elements, and respond to popup dialogs.
>
> To continue training, press <span style="color:#FFFFAA"><kbd>Tab</kbd></span> to toggle mouselook back <span style="color:#AACCFF">on</span> again.

> **Objective:** Open the Field Manual

### Step 10

> This is a complex war, and your <span style="color:#AACCFF">Field Manual</span> has important information you'll need in order to succeed.
>
> Press <span style="color:#FFFFAA"><kbd>H</kbd></span> to view the Field Manual now.

### Step 11: Get to a battle area

> When you are done studying the field manual, press <kbd>Escape</kbd> to close it and continue.

> **Objective:** Get to a battle area

### Step 12

> <span style="color:#AACCFF">Instant Action</span> instantly teleports you to a battle area.  To use it, press <kbd>Escape</kbd> to open the Game Menu, then select Instant Action.  
>
> Another option is to take the <span style="color:#AACCFF">shuttle</span>, which launches every five minutes.
>
> How you get there is your choice, but hurry --we need you at the front!

### Step 13

> <span style="color:#00FF00">Mission Complete!</span>
>
> You're in the field now, and you'll learn quickly through fighting.  
>
> Remember to consult your Field Manual if you get lost.  Better yet, get in a squad with other soldiers, you'll learn much faster that way. 
>
> Good luck, soldier!
