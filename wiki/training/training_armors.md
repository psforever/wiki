# MAX and Infiltration armors

This mission will show you how to use MAX and Infiltration armors, and their special abilities.

**Where:** VR Shooting Range  
**Complete first:** [Weapon Handling](training_weapons01.md)  
**Reward:** 500 battle experience

## Walkthrough

### Step 1

> In addition to Standard, Agile, and Reinforced armors, the Mechanized Assault Exo-Suit (MAX) and Infiltration Suit are also available.
>
> The MAX comes in three configurations, each one designed to combat particular targets. Each empire has its own version of the MAX unit, and each has specialized capabilities unique to that empire.

### Step 2

**New Conglomerate**

> **Objective:** Try the Sparrow MAX armor.

> Go to an Equipment Terminal, click on the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Armor**</span> tab, and equip the Sparrow Config, which is the anti-aircraft MAX for the New Conglomerate.

**Terran Republic**

> **Objective:** Try the Burster MAX armor.

> Go to an Equipment Terminal, click on the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Armor**</span> tab, and equip the Burster Config, which is the anti-aircraft MAX for the Terran Republic.

**Vanu Sovereignty**

> **Objective:** Try the Starfire MAX armor.

> Go to an Equipment Terminal, click on the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Armor**</span> tab, and equip the Starfire Config, which is the anti-aircraft MAX for the Vanu Sovereignty.

### Step 3

> Clear your inventory by clicking on the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Clear**</span> button.

### Step 4

**New Conglomerate**

> Acquire some Sparrow ammunition.

**Terran Republic**

> Acquire some Burster ammunition.

**Vanu Sovereignty**

> Acquire some Starfire ammunition.

### Step 5

> When you are done buying ammunition, close the Equipment Terminal window.

### Step 6

**New Conglomerate**

> NC MAXes have the capability of generating a shield that prevents you from taking damage. However, you cannot fire your weapon while the shield is on.
>
> The shield can be toggled by pressing <kbd>B</kbd>. Try it now.

**Terran Republic**

> TR MAXes have the capability of anchoring down to provide an increased fire rate. However, you become completely immobile while anchored.
>
> The anchors can be engaged by pressing <kbd>B</kbd>. Try it now.

**Vanu Sovereignty**

> VS MAXes have jumpjets providing the ability of limited flight. However, your cone of fire is significantly increased when jumpjets are used.
>
> The jumpjets can be activated by pressing <kbd>B</kbd>. Try it now.

### Step 7

> Your shield strength is represented by the bar currently flashing near your health.
>
> If you fire a weapon while the shield is active, it will automatically deactivate and then activate again a few seconds after you stop firing.
>
> Press <kbd>B</kbd> again to turn the shield off.

### Step 8

> Press <kbd>B</kbd> again to disengage the anchors.

### Step 9

> The power remaining in your jumpjets is represented by the bar currently flashing near your health.  Jumpjets recharge quickly when not in use.
>
> Press the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Next**</span> button to continue.

### Step 10

**New Conglomerate**

> Hold the crosshair over a flight vehicle for a couple seconds to lock on to it.  Sparrow missiles that are fired while locked on will track their target until they hit, the target is destroyed, or they run out of fuel.
>
> Try destroying flying vehicles with the Sparrow weapon.

**Terran Republic**

> The flak projectiles that the Burster fires automatically detonate in proximity to flying vehicles, so it is not necessary to directly hit your target.
>
> Try destroying flying vehicles with the Burster weapon.

**Vanu Sovereignty**

> Hold the crosshair over a flight vehicle and the Starfire will quickly lock on to it.  Starfire projectiles that are fired while locked on will automatically track their target while it stays locked.
>
> Try destroying flying vehicles with the Starfire weapon.

### Step 11

**New Conglomerate**

> When you are ready to continue, return to the equipment terminal and equip the Falcon config - the anti-vehicular MAX for the New Conglomerate.

> **Objective:** Try the Falcon MAX armor.

**Terran Republic**

> When you are ready to continue, return to the equipment terminal and equip the Pounder config - the anti-vehicular MAX for the Terran Republic.
>
> (If you are anchored, you will have to unanchor again by pressing <kbd>B</kbd>)

> **Objective:** Try the Pounder MAX armor.

**Vanu Sovereignty**

> When you are ready to continue, return to the equipment terminal and equip the Comet config - the anti-vehicular MAX for the Vanu Sovereignty.

> **Objective:** Try the Comet MAX armor.

### Step 12

**New Conglomerate**

> Acquire some Falcon ammunition.

**Terran Republic**

> Acquire some Pounder ammunition.

**Vanu Sovereignty**

> Acquire some Comet ammunition.

### Step 13

**New Conglomerate**

> The Falcon's missiles don't track, but they can be fired rapidly to deal a lot of damage to vehicles or to suppress an area.
>
> Try destroying vehicles with the Falcon weapon.

**Terran Republic**

> Pounder grenades have a decent explosion radius, so they don't always have to hit their target to deal damage.
>
> Try destroying vehicles with the Pounder weapon.

**Vanu Sovereignty**

> The Comet projectiles don't pack a huge punch per shot, but they continue to damage their target for a short time after impact.
>
> Try destroying vehicles with the Comet weapon.

### Step 14

**New Conglomerate**

> When you are ready to continue, equip the Scattercannon Config, the New Conglomerate's anti-infantry configuration.

> **Objective:** Try the Scattercannon MAX armor.

**Terran Republic**

> When you are ready to continue, equip the Dual-Cycler Config, the Terran Republic's anti-infantry configuration.

> **Objective:** Try the Dual-Cycler MAX armor.

**Vanu Sovereignty**

> When you are ready to continue, equip the Quasar Config, the Vanu Sovereignty's anti-infantry configuration.

> **Objective:** Try the Quasar MAX armor.

### Step 15

**New Conglomerate**

> Acquire some Scattercannon ammunition.

**Terran Republic**

> Acquire some Dual-Cycler ammunition.

**Vanu Sovereignty**

> Acquire some Quasar ammunition.

### Step 16: Try the Infiltration Suit

> **Objective:** Try the Infiltration Suit.

**New Conglomerate**

> Experiment with the Scattercannon's three fire modes, which are tuned to deal the best damage at different ranges by altering the weapon's shot spread.
>
> Try killing soldiers with the Scattercannon.

**Terran Republic**

> The Dual-Cycler, especially when anchored, is a fearsome anti-infantry weapon.  Little else can cut down enemy soldiers faster than it can.
>
> Try killing soldiers with the Dual-Cycler.

**Vanu Sovereignty**

> The Quasar's strength is its flexibility.  Though it excels at cutting down infantry, it can also do a decent job of suppressing MAXes and vehicles by switching to its secondary fire mode.
>
> Try killing soldiers with the Quasar.

### Step 17

> When you are done trying out the MAXes, let's take a look at the Infiltration Suit.
>
> Equip the Infiltration Suit to continue.

### Step 18

> Close the Equipment Terminal.

### Step 19

> The Infiltration Suit allows you to cloak yourself on the battlefield to avoid visual detection.  It provides no armor protection, has only one pistol holster, and very little inventory space. However, it is extremely useful for scouting under stealth conditions and slipping behind enemy lines to sabotage their equipment.
> By default, the cloak ability is off when you buy it from the equipment terminal.
>
> You may want to switch to third-person view by pressing <kbd>T</kbd> to see the cloak effect.
>
> Toggle cloaking on by pressing <kbd>B</kbd>.

### Step 20

> Notice that performing certain actions make you more visible while cloaking.
> Running, jumping, firing weapons and taking damage will all cause you to glow a bit brighter.
>
> You have now completed all of your armor training.  Press the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Next**</span> button to complete this mission.

### Step 21

> Mission complete.
>
> Congratulations! You have completed the MAX and Infiltration Armor training mission.
