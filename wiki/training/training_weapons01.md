# Weapon Handling

This mission will walk you through some of the basics of PlanetSide, including:
  - How to find the VR Training areas.
  - How to use Equipment Terminals.
  - How to operate weapons.

**Where:** NC Sanctuary, TR Sanctuary, VR Shooting Range, VS Sanctuary  
**Unlocks:** [Certifications](training_certifications.md), [Healing and Repairing](training_healing.md), [Inventory Management](training_inventory.md), [MAX and Infiltration armors](training_armors.md), [Vehicles](training_vehicles.md)  
**Reward:** 500 battle experience

## Walkthrough

### Step 1

> Let's go to the virtual shooting range where you can become acquainted with the weapons arsenal available to you.

### Step 2: Go to the Virtual Shooting Range

> Head outside to find an entrance to one of the virtual training facilities.

> **Objective:** Go to the Virtual Shooting Range

### Step 3

**Vanu Sovereignty**

> Go to one of the virtual training entrances by heading towards the waypoint.
>
> The waypoint is the flashing green triangle that has appeared on your radar display and shows up in the world as a big green beam of light coming down from the sky.

### Step 4: Purchase Reinforced ExoSuit armor

> Go through any of the three doors that lead into the virtual training facility and walk down the corridor.

> Select <span style="color:#72C9D1">**1. Shooting Range**</span>.

> **Objective:** Purchase Reinforced ExoSuit armor.

### Step 5

> This area is the virtual shooting range.  It is a simulation area designed for soldiers to try out all of their empire's available weaponry and armor.
>
> Go to one of the nearby Equipment Terminals and open it.

### Step 6

> This is the Equipment Terminal window.
>
> Here you can acquire different weapons, ammunition, and armor as well as load and save favorite loadouts.  Let's get you set up with an armor type with more inventory space.
>
> Click on the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Armor**</span> tab.

### Step 7: Purchase a Punisher rifle

> Equip the Reinforced ExoSuit by selecting it from the list and clicking the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Acquire**</span> button or by just double-clicking it in the list.

> **Objective:** Purchase a Punisher rifle.

### Step 8

> Notice that this armor provides you with more weapon slots.  You now have two 3x3 pistol sized slots, two 3x9 rifle sized slots, and larger inventory space. 
>
> Before we continue, clear your current inventory by clicking on the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Clear**</span> button in your inventory window.

### Step 9: Purchase ammunition for the Punisher rifle

> Now let's get you set up with a weapon.
>
> Click on the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Weapon**</span> tab.

> Purchase a Punisher rifle.
>
> The Punisher can be found in the Medium Assault section of the weapons menu.

> **Objective:** Purchase ammunition for the Punisher rifle.

### Step 10

> Acquire some ammunition for this weapon.  The ammo each weapon can use is displayed to the right of the weapon icon in the weapon list.  To acquire a box of ammo, just double-click on it. The Punisher can use a wide variety of ammo. Let's try all of them...

> First, acquire a box of 9mm bullets.
>
> This is the standard ammunition for many handheld weapons and is best used against soft targets (like other players).

### Step 11

> Now get two boxes of Armor Piercing 9mm bullets.
>
> These bullets better penetrate vehicles, MAXs, and equipment, but do lower damage to other players compared to the standard 9mm bullets.

### Step 12

> Buy another box of Armor Piercing 9mm bullets to continue.

### Step 13

> Next, get a Rocket Pod.
>
> These rockets, though slow, are fairly accurate and can deliver a good amount of damage to its target.

### Step 14

> Now get a Fragmentation Grenade cartridge.
>
> Frag grenades, like 9mm AP bullets, are best used against vehicles, MAXs, and equipment, but are less effective against soldiers.

### Step 15

> Next, get a Jammer Grenade cartridge.
>
> Jammer grenades are used to disrupt various systems in the game, temporarily disabling turrets, motion sensors, vehicle weapons, and even soldier implants.

### Step 16

> Lastly, acquire a Plasma Grenade cartridge.
>
> Plasma grenades are best used against soldiers and continuously burn their victims over a period of time.

### Step 17

> Now that you're fully equipped, practice using this weapon against some targets.
>
> Close the Equipment Terminal window by hitting the '<span style="color:#FFFFA5">Esc</span>' key or by clicking the '<span style="color:#72C9D1">X</span>' button at the top of the window.

### Step 18: Kill an enemy with the Punisher

> Equip the Punisher by pressing the number that corresponds to it on the weapon HUD.

> **Objective:** Kill an enemy with the Punisher.

### Step 19

> By default, the Punisher is loaded with standard 9mm bullet ammunition, so try killing a soft target (any of the enemy soldiers out there in the shooting range).

### Step 20: Kill an avatar in MAX armor with the Punisher

> Now try some Armor Piercing rounds.  To change the ammunition type in your weapon, press the <kbd>X</kbd> key.
>
> Go ahead and change the ammunition type now.

> **Objective:** Kill an avatar in MAX armor with the Punisher.

### Step 21

> Notice how the weapon display now shows the gold-colored AP ammo box under the Punisher icon.  The upper chat pane also displayed information about the ammo type you switched to.
>
> Kill a MAX unit in the field (one of those big, armored, heavy-looking brutes).

### Step 22: Destroy a vehicle using the Punisher

> Now try out the Punisher's other fire mode.  If you look at the weapon HUD, you will notice that there are two yellow dots to the left of the Punisher icon.  These indicate how many fire modes the weapon has, and which one is currently active.  To change the fire mode, press <kbd>Right Mouse Button</kbd>.
>
> Go ahead and change the fire mode now.

> **Objective:** Destroy a vehicle using the Punisher.

### Step 23

> Notice how the weapon HUD now shows a different ammo type and the second dot is highlighted.  The upper chat pane also displayed information about the changed fire mode.  
> In this fire mode, the Punisher can fire a single rocket or grenade.  Try out the Rocket Pod and various grenades.
>
> Destroy any vehicle with the Punisher to complete this mission.

> Fragmentation grenades do much more damage to vehicles than rockets, but are more difficult to hit targets with since you must take gravity into account.
>
> Destroy any vehicle with the Punisher to complete this mission.

> Jammer grenades do no damage, but they temporary disable vehicle weaponry as well as detonating enemy mines and deactivating the implants of any soldiers caught in their blasts.
>
> Destroy any vehicle with the Punisher to complete this mission.

> Plasma grenades can be very effective against infantry - any soldier caught in their explosions instantly catches on fire and continues to burn for a few seconds.  They are of limited use against vehicles, however.
>
> Destroy any vehicle with the Punisher to complete this mission.

### Step 24

> Mission complete.
>
> Congratulations!  You have completed the Weapon Handling training mission.

>
> Select <span style="color:#72C9D1">**4. Cancel**</span> to continue the mission.

> Mission canceled.
>

> You need to re-equip the Punisher to continue this mission.
