# Using the Map

This mission will get you up to speed on the following:
   - The Proximity Map
   - The Tactical Map
   - The Interstellar Map
   - Spheres of Influence
   - Lattice Links

**Unlocks:** [Using Warpgates](training_warpgates.md), [Using the HART Shuttle](training_hart.md)  
**Reward:** 500 battle experience

## Walkthrough

### Step 1

> The continents we're fighting to control on Auraxis are large, and it's easy to get lost without a map. Fortunately we provide you with maps so you don't stumble into an enemy base asking for directions.
>
> This exercise will train you in using the various maps provided to you.

### Step 2: Familiarize yourself with the Proximity Map

> We'll start with the Proximity map (or radar) window.
>
> Friendly soldiers and vehicles will show up on the Proximity map. Enemy soldiers and vehicles won't always show up on radar unless they're detected by radar, yourself or a squadmate.

> **Objective:** Familiarize yourself with the Proximity Map

### Step 3

> The Proximity map will automatically scale according to your location. If you are outdoors, it will show a wider range, and if you are indoors it will show a closer range.
>
> You can also manually adjust the radius of your Proximity map. You can do so by pressing <kbd>[</kbd> and <kbd>]</kbd>.

### Step 4: Familiarize yourself with the Tactical Map

> While the Proximity map is ideal at showing your local area, there are two other map modes available to you that display tactical and global views. Let's switch to the Tactical Map view. 
>
> Click on the Map button in the Game Windows Menu or press <kbd>M</kbd> to access the Tactical Map View.

> **Objective:** Familiarize yourself with the Tactical Map

### Step 5

> This shows you the full tactical view of the planet you're currently on. Like the Proximity map, you can zoom in and out. This is done by scrolling the mousewheel, or by pressing <kbd>Shift+=</kbd> or <kbd>Numpad Add</kbd> and <kbd>Shift+-</kbd> or <kbd>Numpad Sub</kbd>.
>
> Go ahead and zoom in on the Tactical Map now by scrolling the mousewheel or by pressing <kbd>Shift+=</kbd> or <kbd>Numpad Add</kbd>.

### Step 6

> Excellent! Now try zooming out by scrolling the mousewheel or pressing <kbd>Shift+-</kbd> or <kbd>Numpad Sub</kbd>.

### Step 7

> The map view can be panned in 3 ways:
>
> 1. Moving the mouse to the outer edge of the map
> 2. Grabbing and scrolling the map view while holding down the left mouse button
> 3. Pressing the arrow keys on the keyboard

### Step 8

> You can adjust what you want to see on the map by pressing the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Toggles**</span> button.
>
> Try this now.

### Step 9

> This menu displays the different items that you can toggle the display of. Try each one and see what they do. Your map toggle settings are saved and will load the next time you log in.
>
> When you are ready to continue, press the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Map Legend**</span> button.

### Step 10

> This window lists the different objects that you may see on this map.
>
> When you are ready to continue, click on the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Actions**</span> button.

### Step 11

> This menu lets you change your view of the map. Now let's go over the Global, or Interstellar Map view, which is accessible in this menu, or by pressing <kbd>G</kbd>.
>
> Switch to Interstellar Map view now.

### Step 12: Familiarize yourself with the Interstellar Map

> All of the battle planets, caverns and Empire Sanctuaries are displayed in the Interstellar Map View. 
> This view provides a top-level network system for interplanetary travel. All of the planets are connected via Warpgates, and the Interstellar Map View lets you see the corresponding connection points.

> **Objective:** Familiarize yourself with the Interstellar Map

### Step 13

> If you want to get a better look at a planet, you can double-click on it and it will take you to that planet's Tactical Map.
>
> Look for a planet that has hotspots on it and double-click on it.

### Step 14: Familiarize yourself with SOIs

> One of the first things you'll notice about a battle continent are the colored spheres and the lines that connect them. The spheres are known as Spheres of Influence (SOI), and they're each attached to a facility.

> **Objective:** Familiarize yourself with SOIs

### Step 15

> The name on top of each SOI represents the facility name, followed by the facility type. The SOI color shows who controls that facility.
>
> <span style="color:#1010FF">Blue circles</span> indicate New Conglomerate control.
>
> <span style="color:#FF1010">Red circles</span> indicate Terran Republic control.
>
> <span style="color:#DC00DC">Purple circles</span> indicate Vanu Sovereignty control.
>
> <span style="color:#FFFF00">Yellow circles</span> indicate neutral facilities.

### Step 16

> The symbols that surround a facility indicate its current state and any benefits it may have. To get more detailed information on any facility, double-click on its SOI.
>
> Go ahead and try this now.

### Step 17

> Here you can see detailed information about what those symbols on the SOIs represent. In addition, you get information here that you cannot get elsewhere, such as the time left on a hack, and time left on any installed Modules at that base.
>
> Go ahead and close this window.

### Step 18: Familiarize yourself with Lattice Links

> The colored lines connecting each SOI are Lattice Links. Among other things, these links indicate which facilities can be hacked relative to each other.

> **Objective:** Familiarize yourself with Lattice Links

### Step 19

> A <span style="color:#1010FF">blue line</span> indicates a secure link for the New Conglomerate.
>
> A <span style="color:#FF1010">red line</span> indicates a secure link for the Terran Republic.
>
> A <span style="color:#DC00DC">purple line</span> indicates a secure link for the Vanu Sovereignty.

### Step 20

> A <span style="color:#DCDC00">yellow line</span> indicates a contested link between your empire and an enemy empire.
>
> A <span style="color:#969696">grey line</span> indicates a contested link between two enemy empires.
>
> A solid <span style="color:#10FF10">green line</span> indicates a link to a secure Capitol facility.
>
> A flashing <span style="color:#10FF10">green line</span> indicates a link to a vulnerable Capitol facility.

### Step 21

> The status of Lattice Links also dictate how facility benefits are shared and also determine the state of Broadcast warpgates.
>
> You got a small taste of how vital the map modes can be in navigating your way. Be sure to familiarize yourself with using them as much as possible when you're out on the battlefield.
>
> To complete this training mission, close the map window by pressing '<span style="color:#FFFFA5">Esc</span>'or <kbd>M</kbd>.

### Step 22

> Mission complete.
>
> Congratulations!  You have completed the Map training mission.
