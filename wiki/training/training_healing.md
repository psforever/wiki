# Healing and Repairing

This mission will show you how to use support equipment to heal and repair yourself.

**Where:** VR Shooting Range  
**Complete first:** [Weapon Handling](training_weapons01.md)  
**Reward:** 500 battle experience

## Walkthrough

### Step 1

> When you are in combat, there's a good chance you will take some damage. Fortunately, there are some items and equipment that can be used to prolong your life out on the battlefield.

### Step 2

> Please change your armor to Standard, Agile or Reinforced Exosuit.

### Step 3

> Clear your inventory.

### Step 4: Restore your health using a Medkit

> There are two ways that you can restore your health in the field. The first option is using a Medkit.
>
> Access an Equipment Terminal, click on the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Support**</span> tab, and acquire a Medkit.

> **Objective:** Restore your health using a Medkit

### Step 5

> Notice that you now have a Medkit icon in your shortcut bar that displays how many Medkits you have in your inventory.  You can quickly use a Medkit by pressing the corresponding shortcut key.
>
> Your health has been changed so you can use a Medkit. Close the Equipment Terminal window and try using a Medkit now.

### Step 6: Restore your health using a Medical Applicator

> The other piece of equipment that can restore health in the field is the Medical Applicator.  The Medical Applicator lets you heal other players as well as yourself.
>
> Acquire a Medical Applicator from an Equipment Terminal (available under the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Support**</span> tab).

> **Objective:** Restore your health using a Medical Applicator

### Step 7

> The Medical Applicator is a pistol-sized piece of equipment that fits in any pistol-sized holster.
>
> Close the Equipment Terminal window and equip the Medical Applicator by pressing the key corresponding to the holster you placed the Applicator in.

### Step 8

> The Medical Applicator has two fire modes: Heal and Self Heal.
>
> Switch to Self Heal mode by pressing <kbd>Right Mouse Button</kbd> now.

### Step 9

> Good.  Now you can heal yourself by firing the Applicator just like a normal weapon.
>
> Your health has been changed again. Use the Medical Applicator to restore the remainder of your health.

### Step 10: Repair your armor using a BANK

> Now that your health is fully restored, let's try repairing your armor. This can be done using a Body Armor Nano Kit, or BANK for short.
>
> Acquire a BANK from an Equipment Terminal.

> **Objective:** Repair your armor using a BANK

### Step 11

> The BANK is very similar to the Medical Applicator, but restores armor instead of health.
>
> Close the Equipment Terminal window and equip the BANK.

### Step 12

> The BANK also has two modes: Repair and Self Repair.
>
> Switch to Self Repair mode by pressing <kbd>Right Mouse Button</kbd> now.

### Step 13

> Your armor points have been changed so you can use the BANK. Go ahead and repair your body armor.

### Step 14

> Mission complete.
>
> Congratulations! You have completed the Healing and Repairing mission

## If something goes wrong

> Mission canceled.
>
> Dying has canceled your mission.  Please try again.

> Re-equip the Medical Applicator to continue this mission.

> Re-equip the BANK to continue this mission.

> Leaving the shooting range at this time will cancel this mission.
>
> Select <span style="color:#72C9D1">**4. Cancel**</span> to continue the mission

> Mission canceled.
>
> You have canceled the mission by leaving the shooting range

> This is a Medical Applicator. Please unequip it and use a Medkit to restore your health.
