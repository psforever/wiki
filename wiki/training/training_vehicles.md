# Vehicles

This mission will train you on how to use vehicles, including:
  - Driving ground vehicles
  - Driving hover vehicles
  - Pilot air vehicles
  - Learning how to restock vehicle ammo using the trunk and the Repair/Rearm silo
  - Deploying vehicles
  - Setting vehicle access permissions

**Where:** NC Sanctuary, TR Sanctuary, VR Vehicle Training Area, VS Sanctuary  
**Complete first:** [Inventory Management](training_inventory.md), [Weapon Handling](training_weapons01.md)  
**Reward:** 500 battle experience

## Walkthrough

### Step 1

> Let's go to the VR Vehicle Training Area where you can become acquainted with the vehicles available to you.

### Step 2: Go to the VR Vehicle area

> **Objective:** Go to the VR Vehicle area

**Vanu Sovereignty**

> Go to one of the virtual training entrances by heading towards the waypoint.

### Step 3

> Go through any of the three doors that lead into the virtual training facility and walk down the corridor.

> Select <span style="color:#72C9D1">**2. Vehicle Area**</span>.

### Step 4

> Welcome to the Virtual Reality Vehicle Training Zone.
>
> Begin your training by following the waypoint to the Vehicle Terminal.

### Step 5

> This is a Vehicle Terminal. Just like the Equipment Terminal, you can pick and choose whatever is available to you. Here in VR, you can access all of the vehicles for a test drive.
>
> Go ahead and open the Vehicle Terminal.

### Step 6: Drive a ground vehicle (Harasser)

> Let's start by getting a Harasser buggy.
>
> Scroll down the list until you see the Harasser. Select it and press the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Acquire**</span> button.

> **Objective:** Drive a ground vehicle (Harasser)

### Step 7

> As soon as your vehicle comes up from the bay, it will go into auto-drive mode. After a few seconds you will have full control. Ground vehicles have the following controls:
>
> Accelerate:     <kbd>W</kbd>
> Reverse:     <kbd>S</kbd>
> Turn Left:     <kbd>A</kbd> or <kbd>Left Arrow</kbd>
> Turn Right:     <kbd>D</kbd> or <kbd>Right Arrow</kbd>
> Brake:     <kbd>Space</kbd>
> Switch Camera Views:     <kbd>T</kbd>
>
> Drive to the waypoint up ahead and stop.

### Step 8: Learn the vehicle UI

> **Objective:** Learn the vehicle UI

### Step 9

> Let's talk about the vehicle HUD. On the bottom part of the screen, you will see a speed indicator. The arrow above it indicates your direction. In cruise mode, this arrow becomes filled in.

### Step 10

> On the left side of the screen, you should see a green icon representing your vehicle. This provides you with vehicle information at a quick glance.
>
> A vehicle at full health will be completely green. As it incurs more damage, the vehicle icon will start filling up with red. Keep an eye on this, so you will know when to retreat for repairs or bail out to escape from being caught dead in your vehicle.

### Step 11

> In some situations, your empire might have the benefit of vehicle shields. If you do have this benefit, this icon will glow, letting you know that your vehicle's shields are charging up.

### Step 12

> The yellow circles on this icon represent driver, gunner, and passenger positions. A solid circle means that position is occupied, and an empty circle means it's unoccupied. If a gunner spot is occupied, the dot will also indicate where that gunner is aiming. You can also mouseover the yellow dot and a tooltip will appear with the name of the person in that position.
>
> Let's try getting in a gunner spot now.
>
> Exit the vehicle by pressing <kbd>G</kbd>.

### Step 13

> Walk around to the other side of the vehicle. You should see a pistol-shaped icon, which represents a gunner seat. Some vehicles will have chair-shaped icons. Those represent passenger seats.
>
> Approach the gunner spot and press <kbd>G</kbd> to mount.

### Step 14

> Notice that the gunner spot in the vehicle icon now indicates what direction the weapon is aiming. This information is available to all vehicle occupants. Also notice that there is a weapon HUD along the bottom which indicates your ammo type, fire mode, and ammo remaining. This works just like handheld weapons.
>
> Restocking your vehicle's ammo can be done in two ways. The first way is by manually filling the vehicle's trunk. Let's take a look at the trunk now.
>
> Press <kbd>G</kbd> to exit the vehicle.

### Step 15

> Walk around to the back of the vehicle. You should see another icon there, which represents where the trunk is located for that vehicle. 
>
> Approach the trunk spot and press <kbd>G</kbd> to open it.

### Step 16: Use a Repair/Rearm Silo

> You should now see the trunk window (which varies in size depending on the vehicle), along with your personal inventory window. This works just like lockers, where you can move objects from your inventory into the trunk and vice versa. Another way you can restock on vehicle ammo is by going to a Repair/Rearm Silo.
>
> Get back in the Harasser and drive over to the Repair Silo, indicated by the waypoint.

> **Objective:** Use a Repair/Rearm Silo

### Step 17

> You should now see a prompt to press <kbd>G</kbd> to rearm the vehicle.
>
> Go ahead open the Silo window.

### Step 18

> You should now see the trunk window along with a simplified Equipment Terminal window that only has a tab for vehicle ammo. This works just like normal Equipment Terminal windows, and you can also save vehicle trunk favorites from here as well.
>
> When you are finished, close the Silo window.

### Step 19

> Let's get back and do some more driving. Here are some of the other keyboard controls available to make driving easier:
>
> Full Throttle Cruise:     <kbd>Q</kbd> or <kbd>0</kbd> or <kbd>Numpad 0</kbd>
> Half Throttle Cruise:     <kbd>5</kbd> or <kbd>Numpad 5</kbd>
> Increase Throttle:    <kbd>.</kbd>
> Decrease Throttle:     <kbd>,</kbd>
> Bail:     <kbd>Alt+G</kbd>
>
> Drive along the next few series of waypoints until you reach another vehicle terminal.

### Step 20

> Next we'll try piloting a hover vehicle.
>
> Exit the Harasser.

### Step 21

> Go to the Vehicle Terminal.

### Step 22

> Open the Vehicle Terminal.

### Step 23: Drive a hover vehicle (Switchblade)

> Acquire a Switchblade.

> **Objective:** Drive a hover vehicle (Switchblade)

### Step 24

> The controls for a hover vehicle are slightly different. The mouse is used for turning and the <kbd>Left Arrow</kbd> or <kbd>A</kbd> and <kbd>Right Arrow</kbd> or <kbd>D</kbd> keys now strafe.
>
> Drive the Switchblade towards the waypoints to another Vehicle Terminal.
>
> Accelerate:     <kbd>W</kbd>
> Reverse:     <kbd>S</kbd>
> Strafe Left:     <kbd>Left Arrow</kbd> or <kbd>A</kbd>
> Strafe Right:     <kbd>Right Arrow</kbd> or <kbd>D</kbd>
> Brake:     <kbd>Space</kbd>

### Step 25

> Next we'll try the Advanced Mobile Station (AMS). This vehicle provides special functionality when it is deployed.
>
> Exit the Switchblade.

### Step 26

> Go to the Vehicle Terminal.

### Step 27

> Open the Vehicle Terminal.

### Step 28: Deploy a vehicle (AMS)

> Acquire an Advanced Mobile Station (AMS).

> **Objective:** Deploy a vehicle (AMS)

### Step 29

> Find a place nearby to stop and deploy the vehicle.
>
> When you have picked a location, stop and deploy the vehicle by pressing <kbd>B</kbd>.
>
> If you try to deploy in an invalid location, you will get feedback in the chat pane.

### Step 30

> Exit the AMS.

### Step 31

> You will notice that the AMS is now surrounded by a cloak bubble. Nothing from the outside can see inside the bubble, and vice versa. You will also notice that Equipment Terminals are now available on both sides of the AMS. Around the back is a deconstruction terminal, and up front is a Matrix Panel that can be used to set a bind point at this AMS. All of these features are only available when the AMS is deployed.

### Step 32: Find an Air Vehicle Terminal

> Other vehicles can be deployed as well -- each of them serving a different purpose when deployed. For example, the Switchblade you tried out earlier behaves as a stationary turret when deployed, firing at a faster rate. It would be a good idea to try out the other ground vehicles and their weapons, and get some experience points to boot.
>
> When you are ready to move on, get in any vehicle and follow the waypoints to the Air Vehicle Terminal.

> **Objective:** Find an Air Vehicle Terminal

### Step 33

> Next we'll try piloting a flight vehicle.
>
> Exit your vehicle.

### Step 34

> Go to the Vehicle Terminal.

### Step 35

> Open the Vehicle Terminal.

### Step 36: Pilot an air vehicle

> Acquire a Mosquito.

> **Objective:** Pilot an air vehicle

### Step 37

> The controls for a flight vehicle are similar to a hover vehicle with the addition of elevate and descend controls. Some air vehicles will also have afterburners. When landing aircraft, they must be in a valid spot (relatively flat, and not on structures or other objects).

### Step 38

> Flight vehicles can be rearmed at air vehicle landing pads at each facility, as well as Air Towers.
>
> After you have become familiar with the controls, land and exit the Mosquito to continue with this mission.
>
> Accelerate:     <kbd>W</kbd>
> Reverse:     <kbd>S</kbd>
> Strafe Left:     <kbd>Left Arrow</kbd> or <kbd>A</kbd>
> Strafe Right:     <kbd>Right Arrow</kbd> or <kbd>D</kbd>
> Elevate:     <kbd>E</kbd>
> Descend:     <kbd>C</kbd>
> Afterburners:     <kbd>Space</kbd>
> Bail:     <kbd>Alt+G</kbd>

### Step 39: Learn about vehicle permissions

> Acquire a Galaxy from the Air Vehicle Terminal.

> **Objective:** Learn about vehicle permissions

### Step 40

> As soon as you acquire a vehicle, you become its owner. Ownership of a vehicle lets you control who can access your vehicle.
>
> Find a spot nearby to land the Galaxy and exit the vehicle.

### Step 41

> Open the Vehicle window by clicking on the vehicle icon in the Game Windows Menu, or press <kbd>Alt+V</kbd>.

### Step 42

> The left side of this window contains information on who is occupying your vehicle. If you need to kick someone out of your vehicle, click on that position, and click on the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Kick**</span> button.

### Step 43

> The right side of this window lets you change the access level of each of the vehicle positions: Driver, Gunner, Passenger, and Trunk.

### Step 44

> The access levels available are Locked, Group, and Empire.
>
> <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Locked**</span> locks the position for everyone but yourself.
> <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Group**</span> only allows access to people in your squad, platoon, or outfit.
> <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Empire**</span> allows access to everyone in your empire.

### Step 45

> Sometimes you will want to destroy your vehicle.  As the owner you can do this easily by clicking the <span style="color:#72C9D1">**Deconstruct**</span> button.
>
> Deconstruct your Galaxy to continue.

### Step 46

> That's all there is to know about the vehicle window! Go ahead and try out the other flight vehicles.
>
> When you are ready to move on, go to the Air Vehicle Terminal and acquire any vehicle.

### Step 47

> Go to and land at the waypoint near the BFR terminal.

### Step 48

> Next we'll try piloting a BFR.
>
> Exit the vehicle and follow the waypoint to the BFR Terminal.

### Step 49: Pilot a BFR

> Acquire any BFR from the terminal.

> **Objective:** Pilot a BFR

### Step 50

> The BFR has its own set of unique controls. BFRs support a wide variety of weapons, which can be switched out at a Repair/Rearm Silo. Feel free to try out both BFR types and their weapons.
>
> When you are ready to complete the mission, exit the BFR.
>
> Forward:     <kbd>W</kbd>
> Backward:     <kbd>S</kbd>
> Turn Left:     <kbd>A</kbd> or <kbd>Left Arrow</kbd>
> Turn Right:     <kbd>D</kbd> or <kbd>Right Arrow</kbd>
> Strafe Left:     <kbd>Shift+Q</kbd>
> Strafe Right:     <kbd>Shift+E</kbd>
> Autorun:     <kbd>Q</kbd>
> Flight Elevate:     <kbd>Space</kbd>
> Crouch:     <kbd>C</kbd>

### Step 51

> Mission complete.
>
> Congratulations!  You have completed the Vehicle training mission.
